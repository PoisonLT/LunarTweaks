Privacy Policy

What we collect when you buy and use LunarTweaks, why we need it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated: 25 October 2026 Follows the GDPR

1. Who is responsible

LunarTweaks is responsible (the “controller”) for the personal data described here. You can reach us about anything in this policy at support@lunartweaks.se.

This policy covers lunartweaks.se, buying a Pro or Duo license, and the license checks the LunarTweaks app makes. We collect as little as we can to sell and run licenses.

2. What we collect & why

  • When you buy a license — your email address, the plan you bought and Stripe’s reference for the payment. We use them to create and deliver your license key, help you if you lose it, handle refunds and keep the records the law requires. Legal basis: our contract with you, and our legal obligation to keep accounting records.
  • Your license key — the key itself, its plan and whether it is active or revoked, linked to the email you bought it with. Legal basis: our contract with you.
  • When you activate the app on a PC — a device identifier created by the app, a name for that PC, and when it was activated and last checked its license. We use this to keep to your plan’s PC limit and to let you move your license to a new PC. Legal basis: our contract with you.
  • When you email us — your email address and whatever you write. We use it to answer you. Legal basis: our contract with you, or our legitimate interest in answering questions.
  • Protection against abuse — to stop repeated attempts to guess keys, our key server counts requests per IP address. It stores only a scrambled (salted and hashed) form of the address, never the address itself, and the count is replaced within minutes. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

We don’t sell your data, build profiles of you or use your data for advertising.

3. What stays on your PC

The hardware scan and the profile LunarTweaks builds run on your PC, and their results are not sent to us.

The app doesn’t send diagnostics, crash reports or usage data to us. The only thing it sends is the license check described in section 2.

4. Who we share it with

We use a few services to run LunarTweaks. They handle data for us and only as needed for their part:

  • Stripe — takes your payment. Stripe collects your card details directly; we never see or store them. Stripe also processes payment data under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare — runs our key server and its database, protects the website and forwards email sent to our support address.
  • GitHub — hosts the website.
  • Google — hosts the inbox where support emails arrive.

We may also share data when the law requires it, for example with the Swedish Tax Agency.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Some of these providers are based in the United States, so your data may be processed there. These transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

6. How long we keep it

  • License and PC activation data — for as long as your license can be used, so you can activate it and get it back if you lose it. Freeing a PC removes its activation.
  • Purchase records — 7 years, as the Swedish Bookkeeping Act (bokföringslagen) requires.
  • Support emails — as long as needed to handle your question and any follow-up.
  • Abuse-protection counts — replaced within minutes.

7. Cookies

lunartweaks.se doesn’t use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe’s checkout page uses its own cookies to process payments and prevent fraud; these are covered by Stripe’s policies.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we have about you and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is wrong;
  • delete your data;
  • limit how we use it, or object to uses based on our legitimate interest;
  • give you the data you provided in a machine-readable format.

Email support@lunartweaks.se from the address you bought with, and we’ll answer within one month. Some data we have to keep by law, such as purchase records. If we delete your license data, the key will stop working.

9. Complaints

If you think we handle your data wrongly, please tell us first so we can fix it. You also have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, imy.se), or to the data protection authority where you live.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when LunarTweaks or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change affects how we use data you have already given us, we’ll tell you by email first.
